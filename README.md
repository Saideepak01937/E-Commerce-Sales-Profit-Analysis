# E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of 3,500 e-commerce sales records to understand sales, profit, product performance, and regional trends.

## Tools Used
Excel, Python (Pandas, Google Colab), MySQL, Power BI

## What I Did
- Cleaned and transformed the data using Python and Pandas
- Analyzed sales, profit, product performance, and regional trends using MySQL queries
- Built a 5-page interactive Power BI dashboard (KPIs, monthly sales trends, profitability)
- Compared sales and profit across categories and regions

## Key Insights
- Total sales ~10.67M, profit ~1.84M, profit margin 17.29% across 3.5K orders
- Electronics is the top category (~50% of sales) and the most profitable; Office is the lowest
- West region leads in sales and profit; North is the lowest
- Camera is the top product by sales and profit; Monitor leads in quantity sold
- Sales peak in May and dip in February
- Yearly sales grew from 2022 to 2023, then dipped slightly in 2024
